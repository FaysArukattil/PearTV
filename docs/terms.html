🔓 4. Upstream Open Source Foundations & Attributions

Pear TV is made possible thanks to foundational innovations contributed by open-source creators:

  • CleanYourTV Engine: Pear TV's TV debloating engine, safety classification heuristics, and rollback journal architecture build upon the project by gathin23: github.com/gathin23/cleanyourtv. Local package modifications are dispatched on-device via Shizuku or local ADB without cloud intermediaries.
  • REA (Quick Share Protocol): Pear TV's wireless file transfer receiver implements the open-source Quick Share / Nearby Share protocol by morluto: github.com/morluto/rea. Peer-to-peer data transfers operate directly over local Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) without traversing external networks.
  • Apple Aerial Screensavers: High-definition video screensavers are streamed directly from Apple's public content delivery network servers (a1.phobos.apple.com), providing the iconic tvOS ambient visual experience with OLED burn-in protection.
