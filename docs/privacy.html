Privacy Policy for Pear TV

Last updated: November 17, 2026. Zero telemetry, zero cloud tracking, and complete transparency on every Android TV permission.

🛡️ Zero Data Collection Commitment

Pear TV is an open-source, private, and ad-free launcher for Android TV and Google TV. We firmly believe that what you watch, what apps you install, and what files you transfer on your living room television is strictly your personal business.

Key Guarantee: Pear TV does NOT collect, store, sell, log, or transmit any user data, personal information, device identifiers, IP addresses, usage patterns, or telemetry. There are ZERO analytics SDKs and ZERO advertising libraries bundled in Pear TV.

All core functions — including the app launcher grid, the system debloater, the storage analyzer, the file manager, and the Quick Share receiver — execute 100% locally on your television or across your private local home network.

📲 Quick Share & File Sharing Privacy

Pear TV includes a native implementation of the Quick Share protocol. Here is how your privacy is protected during transfers:

• Zero Internet Dependency: Transfers take place directly over local Wi-Fi or peer-to-peer Direct connections. No files are routed through cloud servers.

• Explicit Consent: Whenever a device attempts to send a file to your TV, Pear TV displays an on-screen consent dialog showing the sender's device name and file preview. Transfers never start without you pressing "Accept".

• Storage Isolation: All accepted files are saved directly into your TV's public Download/ folder or designated media folder for your full control.
