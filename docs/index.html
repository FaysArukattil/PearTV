Refined for Pure Simplicity

Engineered with Apple TV-grade tactile spring physics, remote control D-pad elegance, and complete privacy.

🧹

System App Debloater

Disable vendor bloatware, telemetry, and pre-installed OEM apps locally on your TV via local ADB / Shizuku IPC with a built-in safety rollback journal.

⚡

Pure Speed & Zero Ads

Launches instantaneously. No sponsored movie rows, no banner advertisements, and no tracking scripts hogging your TV's RAM and network.

📲

Local Wireless Quick Share

Built-in Nearby Share receiver. Send APKs, videos, and photos from your Android phone directly to your TV over local Wi-Fi without cloud servers.

📊

Granular Storage Manager

Deep storage breakdown for TVs with constrained space. Inspect per-app cache, APK sizes, and media folders to free up storage with one click.

🍎

4K Aerial Screensavers

Cinematic slow-motion Apple TV aerial flyovers (Dubai, Greenland, San Francisco, London, Manhattan, Hawaii, China) with burn-in protection clock.

📁

Remote-First File Manager

Browse internal storage and connected USB flash drives with your D-pad remote. Sideload APKs and organize files from your couch.

Special Thanks & Open Source Acknowledgements

Pear TV is proudly open source and was made possible by standing on the shoulders of brilliant developers. We want to express our deepest gratitude and courtesy to the creators whose open-source projects directly powered core features of Pear TV:

Feature: System Debloater & App Manager

🧹 CleanYourTV Project

Our sincere thanks to gathin23. Pear TV's system app debloater, safety classification heuristics, local ADB & Shizuku package execution, and automatic rollback journal were built using the proven architecture from the CleanYourTV project.

Visit github.com/gathin23/cleanyourtv →
Feature: Wireless Quick Share Receiver

📲 REA (Quick Share Protocol)

A huge thank you to morluto. Pear TV's wireless file receiving capability was developed using REA's reverse-engineered Quick Share / Nearby Share protocol, enabling direct peer-to-peer file transfers from phones to the TV over local Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

Visit github.com/morluto/rea →
Feature: 4K Aerial Screensavers

🍎 Apple Aerial Screensavers

Heartfelt appreciation to John Coates and the Aerial project community. Pear TV's ambient screensaver mode streams Apple's public tvOS video feeds to deliver 4K landscape flyovers and OLED burn-in protection to the living room.

Visit Aerial Screensaver Project →
