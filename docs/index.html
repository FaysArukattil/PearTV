Built for Living Room Perfection

Every feature is crafted from the ground up for comfortable remote control navigation, maximum speed, and total user privacy.

⚡

Zero Ads & Pure Speed

No sponsored banners, no forced video autoplay, and no unwanted recommendations. Just your apps, launching in under a second with Apple TV-style spatial physics.

📲

Wireless Quick Share

Wirelessly send APKs, movies, videos, and photos from your Android phone or PC directly to your TV. Zero setup, fast local Wi-Fi transfer, and full on-screen consent prompts.

🧹

1-Click TV Debloater

Disable or uninstall stubborn pre-installed manufacturer bloatware that hogs storage and RAM. Comes with an automatic rollback journal so you can re-enable apps anytime.

📊

Detailed Storage Manager

Visualize your internal storage with color-coded badges for Apps, Cache, and System data. Clean cached files in 1 click and eliminate "Storage Full" TV alerts.

🍎

4K Aerial Screensavers

Stunning, slow-motion Apple TV aerial flyovers (Dubai, Greenland, San Francisco, London, Manhattan, Liwa, China) with live burn-in protection clock and remote skip controls.

📁

Remote-First File Manager

Explore internal storage and connected USB flash drives with your D-pad remote. 1-click install sideloaded APKs and manage your media library from your couch.

Pear TV is an independent community project. There are no paid tiers, no trackers, and no hidden telemetry. Check out our public repository, inspect the code, or contribute on GitHub.
