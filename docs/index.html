Built Different From Every OEM Launcher

🧹

Built-in Safe Debloater

Powered by the CleanYourTV engine. Safely freeze or disable carrier and OEM bloatware directly from your remote via local ADB or Shizuku, with zero risk of bricking your device.

📲

Wireless Quick Share

Integrated REA engine turns your TV into an authentic Nearby Share / Quick Share receiver. Send photos, videos, and files directly from your phone with no third-party cloud apps.

4K Apple Aerials

Experience Apple TV's iconic 4K drone cinematography. Streams verified Apple CDN feeds with location tags and OLED burn-in protection after idle periods.

True #000000 dark canvas with zero sponsored banners, zero recommendation feeds, and zero telemetry. Instant remote responsiveness with tvOS-grade physics.
